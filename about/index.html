Our mission

MDOT plans, builds, and maintains a safe, reliable transportation network that connects Michigan's communities, supports its economy, and keeps people and goods moving — from state highways and bridges to public transit and rail.

That work touches nearly every part of daily life: the commute to work, the school bus route, the freight truck delivering goods, and the bridge you cross without a second thought.

02

What we do

  • Roads & bridges. Design, build, and maintain state highways, freeways, and bridges across Michigan.
  • Construction & permits. Manage active construction projects and issue permits for oversize loads, driveways, and utility work.
  • Transit. Fund and coordinate public transit, including local agencies and intercity bus service.
  • Safety. Run statewide safety campaigns and design work zones, crossings, and roadways with driver, worker, and pedestrian safety in mind.
  • Business & contracts. Manage bidding, vendor registration, and contracts for the construction and maintenance work that keeps Michigan moving.
03

How we're organized

MDOT operates through a central office and regional teams that manage day-to-day work close to the communities they serve. Flint falls under the University Region, which covers Genesee County and the surrounding area — handling everything from local road maintenance coordination to construction project oversight.

04

Our history

1905

Michigan establishes a state highway department to coordinate road building across the growing state.

1950s–60s

Michigan builds out its interstate highway system, connecting major cities including Flint, Detroit, and Grand Rapids.

1970s

The department is renamed the Michigan Department of Transportation, expanding its mission beyond highways to include transit and rail.

Today

MDOT continues to modernize aging infrastructure, expand transit access, and improve safety across the state's roads and bridges.
